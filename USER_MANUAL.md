## Introduction

### Welcome to ActiveX Gym App

ActiveX Gym App is a comprehensive mobile fitness application designed to help you achieve your fitness goals. This single mobile app supports three different user roles (User, Trainer, and Admin), each with their own interface and features. Whether you're a fitness enthusiast, a personal trainer, or a gym administrator, you all use the same mobile app - the app automatically shows you the appropriate interface based on your account role.

### Key Features

- **Personalized Dashboard**: Track your workout progress and view today's workouts
- **Workout Plans**: Access and follow structured workout plans created by trainers
- **Exercise Library**: Browse exercises with video demonstrations
- **Progress Tracking**: Log your daily weight, calories burned, and fitness metrics
- **Nutrition Plans**: Follow meal plans designed by trainers
- **Community Challenges**: Participate in fitness challenges with other users
- **Role-Based Access**: Same mobile app with different interfaces automatically shown based on your account role (User, Trainer, or Admin)

### System Requirements

- **Mobile Device**: Android or iOS smartphone or tablet
- **Internet Connection**: Required for initial setup and data synchronization
- **Operating System**: 
  - Android 5.0 (API level 21) or higher
  - iOS 12.0 or higher

### Important: Single Mobile Application

**ActiveX Gym App is ONE mobile application** that serves all user types:
- ✅ **One app** for everyone (Users, Trainers, and Admins)
- ✅ **One login screen** for all roles
- ✅ **Role-based interface** - the app automatically shows you the right features based on your account type
- ❌ **NOT separate apps** - you don't need different apps for different roles
- ❌ **NOT a web portal** - this is a mobile application only

When you log in, the app detects your role and shows the appropriate interface:
- **Users** → Standard user interface (Home, Workouts, Progress, Profile)
- **Trainers** → Trainer interface (Plans, Nutrition, Challenges, Exercises, Profile)
- **Admins** → Admin interface (User Management page)

## Getting Started

### Installation

1. **Download the App**
   - For Android: Download the APK file and install it on your device
   - For iOS: Install from the App Store (when available)

2. **Launch the App**
   - Tap the ActiveX Gym App icon on your device
   - The app will open to the Login screen

### Creating Your Account

1. **Access Registration**
   - On the Login screen, tap the **"Sign Up"** link at the bottom
   - You'll be taken to the Registration page

2. **Fill in Your Information**
   - **Full Name**: Enter your complete name
   - **Email**: Enter a valid email address (this will be your login username)
   - **Password**: Create a password (minimum 8 characters)
   - **Terms & Conditions**: Check the box to agree to Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy

3. **Complete Registration**
   - Tap the **"Sign Up"** button
   - You'll be automatically logged in and taken to the Home screen

### Logging In

1. **Enter Your Credentials**
   - Open the app to see the Login screen
   - Enter your registered email address
   - Enter your password
   - Tap the eye icon to show/hide your password if needed

2. **Sign In**
   - Tap the **"Sign In"** button
   - The app will verify your credentials and automatically detect your role
   - You'll be taken to the appropriate interface:
     - **Users**: Home screen with bottom navigation (Home, Workouts, Progress, Profile)
     - **Trainers**: Trainer dashboard with bottom navigation (Plans, Nutrition, Challenges, Exercises, Profile)
     - **Admins**: Admin User Management page directly

3. **Forgot Password?**
   - Tap **"Forgot Password?"** link (if available)
   - Follow the instructions to reset your password

### Understanding User Roles

ActiveX Gym App is a single mobile application that supports three different user roles. Everyone uses the same app and login screen, but the interface and features you see depend on your account role:

- **User**: Regular fitness enthusiasts who can view plans, track progress, and participate in challenges. After login, you'll see the standard user interface with Home, Workouts, Progress, and Profile tabs.

- **Trainer**: Fitness professionals who can create workout plans, nutrition plans, exercises, and challenges. After login, you'll see the trainer interface with Plans, Nutrition, Challenges, Exercises, and Profile tabs.

- **Admin**: Administrators who manage users and trainers in the system. After login, you'll be taken directly to the Admin User Management page.

**Important**: All roles use the same mobile app and login screen. The app automatically detects your role and shows the appropriate interface. You don't need separate apps or portals - it's all role-based access within the same application.

## User Guide

### Home Screen

The Home screen is your main dashboard, providing an overview of your fitness journey.

#### Features on Home Screen:

1. **Header Section**
   - Personalized greeting (e.g., "Good Morning")
   - Your name displayed
   - Notification icon (red dot indicates new notifications)
   - Menu icon for additional options

2. **Workout Progress Card**
   - Circular progress indicator showing your overall workout completion
   - Percentage of workouts completed
   - Number of exercises remaining

3. **Quick Links**
   - **Challenges**: Tap to view community challenges
   - **Nutrition**: Tap to view your nutrition plans
   - **Logs**: Tap to view and manage your progress logs

4. **Today's Workouts**
   - Displays workouts scheduled for today
   - Shows workout duration and calorie information
   - Tap any workout card to view details

5. **Popular Exercises**
   - Browse popular exercise categories
   - Tap any exercise to view details and video demonstrations

#### Navigation

- Use the **bottom navigation bar** to switch between:
  - **Home** (house icon)
  - **Workouts** (dumbbell icon)
  - **Progress** (chart icon)
  - **Profile** (person icon)

### Workout Plans

#### Viewing Workout Plans

1. **Access Workout Plans**
   - Tap **"Workouts"** in the bottom navigation bar, OR
   - Navigate from the Home screen quick links

2. **Browse Plans**
   - View all available workout plans in a scrollable list
   - Each plan card shows:
     - Plan name and description
     - Difficulty level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
     - Progress percentage
     - Duration (weeks) and sessions per week
     - Start date

3. **Filter Plans**
   - Use filter chips at the top: **All Plans**, **Active**, **Completed**, **Drafts**
   - Tap a filter to view plans by status

#### Viewing Workout Plan Details

1. **Open a Plan**
   - Tap any workout plan card to view details

2. **Plan Information**
   - **Header**: Plan name and difficulty badge
   - **Trainer**: Name of the trainer who created the plan
   - **Metrics**: Difficulty level and number of workouts
   - **About Plan**: Full description of the plan
   - **Schedule**: Day-by-day workout breakdown with sets and reps
   - **Exercises**: List of exercises included in the plan

3. **Exercise Details**
   - Tap any exercise card to view:
     - Exercise name and difficulty
     - Sets and reps information
     - Target muscles
     - Equipment needed
     - Video demonstration link
   - Tap the star icon to add/remove exercise from favorites

4. **Start a Workout**
   - From exercise detail page, you can access the Exercise Player
   - Follow along with sets, reps, and rest timers

### Exercises

#### Browsing Exercises

1. **Access Exercises**
   - Exercises are available within workout plans
   - Tap an exercise from a workout plan detail page

2. **Exercise Information**
   - Exercise name and difficulty level
   - Sets and reps configuration
   - Rest time between sets
   - Target muscle groups
   - Equipment required
   - Video URL for demonstration

3. **Using Exercise Player**
   - Tap to start the Exercise Player
   - Follow the guided workout:
     - Complete sets as indicated
     - Rest timer counts down between sets
     - Pause/Resume rest timer if needed
     - Mark sets as complete
   - Tap **"Finish"** when all sets are completed

### Progress Logging

#### Creating a Progress Log

1. **Access Progress Logs**
   - Tap **"Logs"** quick link on Home screen, OR
   - Tap **"Progress"** in bottom navigation, then navigate to Logs

2. **Add New Log**
   - Tap the **"+"** button (Floating Action Button or App Bar icon)
   - The Progress Log Form will open

3. **Fill in Log Information**
   - **Date**: Tap the date field to select a date (defaults to today)
   - **Weight (kg)**: Enter your current weight (e.g., 70.5)
   - **Calories Burned**: Enter calories burned during workouts (e.g., 500)
   - **Notes (Optional)**: Add any additional notes about your progress

4. **Save Log**
   - Tap **"Save Progress Log"** button
   - You'll be returned to the Progress Logs list
   - Your new log will appear at the top

#### Viewing Progress Logs

1. **Log List**
   - All your progress logs are displayed in chronological order
   - Each log shows:
     - Date (formatted as "Today", "Yesterday", or full date)
     - Weight in kilograms
     - Calories burned
     - Notes (if provided)

2. **Edit a Log**
   - Tap any log card to open it in edit mode
   - Modify any fields
   - Tap **"Update Progress Log"** to save changes

3. **Delete a Log**
   - Tap the menu icon (three dots) on a log card
   - Select **"Delete"**
   - Confirm deletion in the dialog
   - The log will be removed from your list

### Nutrition Plans

#### Viewing Nutrition Plans

1. **Access Nutrition Plans**
   - Tap **"Nutrition"** quick link on Home screen, OR
   - Navigate from the main menu

2. **Browse Plans**
   - View all assigned nutrition plans
   - Each plan card shows:
     - Plan name
     - Daily calories target (e.g., "2000 kcal")
     - Diet type (Balanced, Keto, Vegan)
     - Status (Active, Completed, Draft)
     - Duration in weeks
     - Progress percentage

3. **Plan Details**
   - Tap a plan card to view full details
   - See meal schedule and nutritional information

### Challenges

#### Viewing Challenges

1. **Access Challenges**
   - Tap **"Challenges"** quick link on Home screen, OR
   - Navigate from the main menu

2. **Browse Challenges**
   - View all available community challenges
   - Each challenge card shows:
     - Challenge title and description
     - Number of participants
     - Average progress percentage
     - Progress bar visualization

3. **Challenge Details**
   - Tap a challenge card to view details (if detail page is available)
   - See start and end dates
   - View participants and leaderboard
   - Track your own progress

#### Joining a Challenge

1. **Join Action**
   - On a challenge card, tap **"Join Challenge"** button (if available)
   - You'll be added as a participant with 0% initial progress

2. **Update Progress**
   - Navigate to challenge detail page
   - Update your progress percentage
   - Your progress will be reflected in the challenge statistics

### Profile

#### Accessing Your Profile

1. **Open Profile**
   - Tap **"Profile"** icon in the bottom navigation bar

2. **Profile Information**
   - **Header Section**:
     - Your profile picture (default icon)
     - Your name
     - Member status badge
     - Statistics: Workouts completed, Calories burned, Current streak

3. **Quick Actions**
   - **My Goals**: View and manage your fitness goals
   - **Welcome**: Access welcome information

4. **Account Settings**
   - **Settings**: App preferences and configurations
   - **Notifications**: Manage alert settings
   - **Health Data**: Connect fitness devices
   - **Achievements**: View all badges and accomplishments
   - **Friends**: Invite and connect with friends
   - **Help & Support**: Get assistance and support

5. **Sign Out**
   - Scroll to the bottom
   - Tap **"Sign Out"** button
   - Confirm to log out and return to Login screen

## Trainer Guide

### Trainer Dashboard

**Note**: Trainers use the same ActiveX Gym App mobile application as regular users. When you log in with trainer credentials, the app automatically shows you the trainer interface instead of the standard user interface.

Trainers have access to additional features for creating and managing fitness content.

#### Trainer Navigation

After logging in as a trainer, you'll see a different bottom navigation bar with 5 main sections:
- **Plans**: Workout plans management
- **Nutrition**: Nutrition plans management
- **Challenges**: Community challenges management
- **Exercises**: Exercise library management
- **Profile**: Trainer profile and settings

The trainer interface uses a purple color scheme to distinguish it from the user interface (which uses green).

### Creating Workout Plans

1. **Access Workout Plans**
   - Tap **"Plans"** in the trainer bottom navigation

2. **Create New Plan**
   - Tap **"Create New Workout Plan"** button
   - The Workout Plan Form will open

3. **Fill in Basic Information**
   - **Plan Name***: Enter a descriptive name (e.g., "Beginner Full Body")
   - **Assign to Client***: Select a user from the dropdown
   - **Duration (weeks)**: Enter plan duration (default: 4 weeks)
   - **Difficulty**: Select Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Fitness Goal***: Describe the goal (e.g., "Build muscle, lose weight")
   - **Duration (min)**: Estimated workout duration in minutes
   - **Calories (kcal)**: Estimated calories burned
   - **Tags**: Add tags (e.g., "Cardio", "Full Body") - tap "+" to add each tag
   - **Equipment**: List required equipment
   - **Workout Image**: Tap to select an image from gallery

4. **Build Workout Schedule**
   - **Add Day**: Tap **"Add Day"** to add workout days
   - **Add Exercise**: Within each day, tap **"Add Exercise"**
   - For each exercise:
     - Select exercise from dropdown
     - Enter number of sets
     - Enter reps (e.g., "10" or "10-12")
     - Enter rest time in seconds
   - Remove exercises or days using the delete icons

5. **Save Plan**
   - Tap **"Create Workout Plan"** button at the bottom
   - The plan will be saved and assigned to the selected client

#### Editing Workout Plans

1. **Edit Existing Plan**
   - On the Workout Plans page, tap the menu icon (three dots) on a plan card
   - Select **"Edit"**
   - Modify any fields
   - Tap **"Update Workout Plan"** to save changes

2. **Delete Plan**
   - Tap the menu icon on a plan card
   - Select **"Delete"**
   - The plan will be removed

### Creating Nutrition Plans

1. **Access Nutrition Plans**
   - Tap **"Nutrition"** in trainer bottom navigation

2. **Create New Plan**
   - Tap **"Create New Meal Plan"** button
   - The Nutrition Plan Form will open

3. **Fill in Information**
   - **Plan Name***: Enter a descriptive name (e.g., "High Protein Diet")
   - **Assign to Client***: Select a user from dropdown
   - **Duration (weeks)**: Enter plan duration
   - **Daily Calories**: Enter target daily calories (default: 2000)
   - **Diet Type**: Select Balanced, Keto, or Vegan
   - **Nutrition Goal***: Describe the goal (e.g., "Muscle gain, weight loss")

4. **Build Meal Schedule**
   - **Add Day**: Tap **"Add Day"** to add meal days
   - **Add Meal**: Within each day, tap **"Add Meal"**
   - For each meal:
     - Select meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack)
     - Enter meal name
     - Enter description/ingredients
     - Enter calories, protein (g), carbs (g), fats (g)

5. **Save Plan**
   - Tap **"Save Meal Plan"** button
   - The plan will be saved and assigned to the selected client

#### Editing Nutrition Plans

1. **Edit Plan**
   - Tap the menu icon on a nutrition plan card
   - Select **"Edit"**
   - Modify fields in the dialog
   - Tap **"Save"** to update

2. **Delete Plan**
   - Tap the menu icon
   - Select **"Delete"**
   - Confirm deletion

### Managing Exercises

1. **Access Exercise Management**
   - Tap **"Exercises"** in trainer bottom navigation

2. **Create New Exercise**
   - Tap **"New Exercise"** Floating Action Button
   - The Exercise Form will open

3. **Fill in Exercise Details**
   - **Exercise Name***: Enter exercise name (e.g., "Push-ups")
   - **Difficulty**: Select Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Sets**: Enter default number of sets
   - **Reps**: Enter default reps (e.g., "10" or "10-12")
   - **Rest Time (seconds)**: Enter rest time between sets
   - **Target Muscles**: Add target muscle groups
   - **Video URL**: Enter link to exercise demonstration video

4. **Save Exercise**
   - Tap **"Save Exercise"** button
   - Exercise will be added to the library

#### Editing and Deleting Exercises

1. **Edit Exercise**
   - Tap the menu icon (three dots) on an exercise card
   - Select **"Edit"**
   - Modify fields
   - Tap **"Update Exercise"** to save

2. **Delete Exercise**
   - Tap the menu icon
   - Select **"Delete"**
   - Confirm deletion in the dialog
   - Exercise will be removed from library

### Creating Challenges

1. **Access Challenges**
   - Tap **"Challenges"** in trainer bottom navigation

2. **Create New Challenge**
   - Tap **"Create"** Floating Action Button
   - The Challenge Form will open

3. **Fill in Challenge Information**
   - **Title***: Enter challenge name (e.g., "30-Day Fitness Challenge")
   - **Description**: Enter challenge description
   - **Start Date**: Tap to select start date (defaults to today)
   - **End Date**: Tap to select end date (defaults to 30 days from start)

4. **Save Challenge**
   - Tap **"Save Challenge"** button
   - Challenge will be created and you'll be added as first participant

#### Editing and Deleting Challenges

1. **Edit Challenge**
   - Tap the menu icon on a challenge card
   - Select **"Edit"**
   - Modify title, description, or dates in the dialog
   - Tap **"Save"** to update

2. **Delete Challenge**
   - Tap the menu icon
   - Select **"Delete"**
   - Challenge will be removed immediately

### Trainer Profile

1. **Access Profile**
   - Tap **"Profile"** in trainer bottom navigation

2. **View Profile Information**
   - Trainer name and title
   - Bio and credentials

   - Statistics (clients, plans created, rating)

   - Update information
   - Save changes

## Admin Guide

### Admin Dashboard

**Note**: Administrators use the same ActiveX Gym App mobile application. When you log in with admin credentials, the app automatically detects your role and shows you the admin interface.

Administrators manage users and trainers in the system.

### Accessing Admin Features

1. **Login as Admin**
   - Open the ActiveX Gym App (same app as users and trainers)
   - On the Login screen, enter admin credentials
   - Email: Use an email containing "admin" or "admin@gmail.com"
   - Enter your password
   - Tap **"Sign In"**
   - The app will automatically detect you're an admin and take you directly to the Admin User Management page
   - You won't see the standard user or trainer interface - only admin features

### Managing Users

1. **View Users**
   - The Admin User Management page opens automatically
   - **Users** tab shows all regular users (role = 'user')
   - **Trainers** tab shows all trainers (role = 'trainer')

2. **Switch Between Tabs**
   - Tap **"Users"** or **"Trainers"** segmented buttons at the top
   - Lists will filter accordingly

3. **View User Information**
   - Each user/trainer card shows:
     - Profile icon
     - Name
     - Email address
     - Delete button

### Creating Trainer Accounts

1. **Create New Trainer**
   - Switch to **"Trainers"** tab
   - Tap **"New Trainer"** Floating Action Button
   - Fill in the dialog:
     - **Name**: Enter trainer's full name
     - **Email**: Enter trainer's email address
     - **Password**: Create a password for the trainer
   - Tap **"Create"** button
   - Trainer account will be created and appear in the Trainers list

2. **Trainer Login**
   - New trainers can log in using the email and password you created
   - They'll have access to trainer features

### Deleting Users or Trainers

1. **Delete Account**
   - Find the user or trainer in the list
   - Tap the **delete icon** (trash can) on their card
   - The account will be immediately removed from the system

2. **Confirmation**
   - Currently, deletion is immediate (no confirmation dialog)
   - Be careful when deleting accounts

### Logging Out as Admin

1. **Sign Out**
   - Tap the **logout icon** (door icon) in the top-right corner
   - You'll be signed out and returned to the Login screen

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Login Problems

**Issue**: Cannot log in with correct credentials
- **Solution**: 
  - Verify email address is correct (check for typos)
  - Ensure password is entered correctly
  - Try tapping the eye icon to verify password
  - If you're a new user, make sure you've registered first
  - For trainers created by admin, use the exact email and password provided

**Issue**: "No user account found" error
- **Solution**:
  - Make sure you've registered an account first
  - Check that you're using the correct email address
  - Try registering again if account doesn't exist

#### Data Not Loading

**Issue**: Lists show loading spinner indefinitely
- **Solution**:
  - Check your internet connection
  - Close and reopen the app
  - Pull down to refresh the list (if pull-to-refresh is available)
  - Restart your device if problem persists

**Issue**: Empty lists when data should exist
- **Solution**:
  - Verify you're logged in with the correct account
  - Check if data was created by a trainer/admin
  - Try logging out and logging back in

#### Form Submission Issues

**Issue**: Cannot save progress log or create plan
- **Solution**:
  - Check that all required fields (marked with *) are filled
  - Verify data formats are correct:
    - Weight: Enter number (e.g., 70.5)
    - Calories: Enter whole number (e.g., 500)
    - Dates: Select valid dates
  - Ensure you have proper permissions (trainers can create plans, users can create logs)

**Issue**: Validation errors appear
- **Solution**:
  - Read the error message carefully
  - Fill in the required field that's showing an error
  - Check that values are in correct format (e.g., weight > 0, calories >= 0)

#### Navigation Issues

**Issue**: Cannot navigate between screens
- **Solution**:
  - Use the bottom navigation bar for main sections
  - Use back button (top-left or device back button) to go back
  - Tap cards to navigate to detail pages
  - If stuck, close and reopen the app

**Issue**: Bottom navigation not visible
- **Solution**:
  - Scroll up if you're scrolled down on a page
  - Check if you're on a full-screen page (some pages hide navigation)
  - Return to main screen using back button

#### Performance Issues

**Issue**: App is slow or laggy
- **Solution**:
  - Close other apps running in background
  - Restart your device
  - Check available storage space
  - Update the app if a new version is available

**Issue**: Images not loading
- **Solution**:
  - Check internet connection
  - Wait a few seconds for images to load
  - Try refreshing the page
  - Some images may be placeholders if original URLs are unavailable

### Getting Help

If you encounter issues not covered here:

1. **Check FAQ Section**: See Frequently Asked Questions below
2. **Contact Support**: Use Help & Support in Profile settings
3. **Report Bug**: Contact app administrators with:
   - Description of the issue
   - Steps to reproduce
   - Screenshots if possible
   - Device and app version information

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Account and Login

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Tap "Sign Up" on the Login screen, fill in your name, email, and password, accept terms, and tap "Sign Up". You'll be automatically logged in.

**Q: Can I change my email address?**
A: Currently, email changes are not supported. Contact support if you need to change your email. Note: For trainers created by admins, contact your administrator to update your account information.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: Tap "Forgot Password?" on the Login screen (if available) and follow the instructions. Otherwise, contact support.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: Yes, you can create multiple accounts with different email addresses.

### Workout Plans

**Q: How do I get assigned a workout plan?**
A: Trainers create and assign workout plans to users. Contact a trainer or wait for a plan to be assigned to you.

**Q: Can I create my own workout plan?**
A: Regular users cannot create workout plans. Only trainers and admins can create plans. Consider becoming a trainer or ask a trainer to create a plan for you.

**Q: How do I track my progress in a workout plan?**
A: Use the Progress Logs feature to log your daily weight, calories burned, and other metrics. Your progress will be reflected in the plan's progress bar.

**Q: What if I can't complete a workout?**
A: You can skip workouts or adjust them as needed. Log your actual progress in Progress Logs.

### Exercises

**Q: Where can I find exercise videos?**
A: Exercise videos are available when you view exercise details from a workout plan. Tap an exercise card to see the video URL.

**Q: How do I add exercises to favorites?**
A: When viewing an exercise in a workout plan detail page, tap the star icon to add it to favorites. The star will fill with color when favorited.

**Q: Can I create custom exercises?**
A: Only trainers can create exercises. Regular users can view and use exercises created by trainers.

### Progress Logging

**Q: How often should I log my progress?**
A: You can log progress daily, weekly, or as often as you like. Daily logging provides the most detailed tracking.

**Q: Can I log progress for past dates?**
A: Yes, when creating a progress log, tap the date field and select any past date up to today.

**Q: What if I make a mistake in a log entry?**
A: Tap the log entry to edit it, make corrections, and tap "Update Progress Log" to save changes.

**Q: Can I delete old progress logs?**
A: Yes, tap the menu icon (three dots) on any log and select "Delete". Confirm deletion in the dialog.

### Nutrition Plans

**Q: How do I get a nutrition plan?**
A: Trainers create and assign nutrition plans to users. Contact a trainer or wait for a plan to be assigned.

**Q: Can I modify a nutrition plan?**
A: Regular users cannot modify plans. Contact your trainer if you need adjustments to your plan.

**Q: Do nutrition plans include recipes?**
A: Plans include meal names, descriptions, and nutritional information. Detailed recipes may be included in meal descriptions.

### Challenges

**Q: How do I join a challenge?**
A: Navigate to Challenges, find a challenge you want to join, and tap "Join Challenge" button (if available). You'll start with 0% progress.

**Q: How do I update my challenge progress?**
A: Open the challenge detail page and update your progress percentage. Your progress will be reflected in the challenge statistics.

**Q: Can I leave a challenge?**
A: If the leave functionality is available, use the "Leave Challenge" button on the challenge detail page.

**Q: What happens if I don't complete a challenge?**
A: Your progress will remain recorded. You can continue working on the challenge or join new ones.

### Trainer Features

**Q: How do I become a trainer?**
A: Contact an administrator to have a trainer account created for you. Admins can create trainer accounts from within the same mobile app using the Admin User Management page. Once your trainer account is created, you log in to the same ActiveX Gym App, and it will automatically show you the trainer interface.

**Q: Do I need a separate app to be a trainer?**
A: No, trainers use the same ActiveX Gym App mobile application. The app automatically shows the trainer interface when you log in with trainer credentials. You don't need a separate app or portal.

**Q: Can I assign plans to multiple users?**
A: Yes, when creating a workout or nutrition plan, you can select different users from the "Assign to Client" dropdown for each plan.

**Q: How do I delete a plan I created?**
A: On the Plans or Nutrition page, tap the menu icon (three dots) on the plan card and select "Delete".

**Q: Can I edit exercises I created?**
A: Yes, tap the menu icon on any exercise card and select "Edit" to modify exercise details.

### Admin Features

**Q: How do I access admin features?**
A: Log in to the same ActiveX Gym App mobile application with an admin account (email containing "admin" or "admin@gmail.com"). The app will automatically detect your admin role and take you directly to the Admin User Management page. You don't need a separate admin app or web portal.

**Q: Do admins use a different app?**
A: No, administrators use the same ActiveX Gym App mobile application as users and trainers. The app uses role-based access control - when you log in as an admin, it automatically shows you the admin interface instead of the user or trainer interface.

**Q: How do I create a trainer account?**
A: As an admin, log in to the ActiveX Gym App mobile application. You'll be taken to the Admin User Management page. Switch to the "Trainers" tab, tap "New Trainer" button, fill in the trainer's name, email, and password, then tap "Create". The trainer can then log in to the same mobile app using those credentials, and the app will automatically show them the trainer interface.
